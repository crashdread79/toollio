Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and TIFF formats. Most modern image files work perfectly.

Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 50MB. For most use cases, this is more than sufficient.

Will my image quality suffer?

Our algorithm intelligently balances quality and file size. For most targets, the difference is imperceptible. We recommend using the "Auto" preset for best results.

Does this tool work on mobile?

Yes! Image Reducer is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Are my images uploaded to your servers?

No. All image processing happens in your browser. Your images never leave your device.
